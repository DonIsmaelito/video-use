These five agent-authored prompt trials exercise the motion library through distinct creative problems. Each short prompt was expanded into art direction, geometry or image work, choreography, sound and rendered repairs. They are examples of an iterative authoring workflow, not a measured autonomous benchmark, a model ranking, or evidence of native image-to-video generation.

The shared contract is eight seconds at 1920×1080/30fps, original sound, editable source, and a hero that carries the film. No supporting copy, corner labels, UI or decorative framing belongs in these examples. MELT uses the requested word as its hero. The [machine-readable examples](../skills/motion-design/library/prompt-examples.json) preserve exact prompts and links to original recipe documentation and demos; the [catalog](../skills/motion-design/library/catalog.json) remains the source of reference and asset records.

**“Make a red paper koi unfold from a square and swim through blue ink ripples.”**

The expansion gives the fish a broad carp silhouette, forked tail, paired fins and barbels on an ivory paper field. Original Canvas facets and seeded fibers supply artwork; one body deformation also moves its attachments. The film owns the swim path and blue ripple events. Selected records: `ordinary-folk-dreams`, `mediawork-office-motion-tests`, `mediawork-pinterest-objects`. The resulting [paper-koi recipe](../skills/motion-design/library/recipes/paper-koi.md) exposes construction and swimming controls. Proof revealed an overly oval construction pose, faint ink and tight upper framing; a spine-hinge fold, stronger ripples and a lower path addressed those findings. It is expressive paper illustration, not a valid origami simulation.

**“Make a cherry-red jelly chair land, squash, and spring back into shape.”**

A continuous horseshoe back, cushion and four supports establish the chair before a grounded squash and diminishing rebounds. A peach studio and broad HDR reflections distinguish this material study. Selected records: `mediawork-pinterest-objects`, `polyhaven-studio-small-09-1k`. The [jelly-chair recipe](../skills/motion-design/library/recipes/jelly-chair.md) owns the original geometry and absolute deformation. Proof exposed a pinched back, hard shadow and white reflections that weakened the cherry color; the author repaired the back curve, softened shadows and moderated reflections.

**“Make a chrome beetle open its wing cases and take flight.”**

An authored insect has engraved elytra, six articulated legs, antennae and amber veined membranes. Rigid cases open first; membrane panels unfold, flutter and support a controlled lift. Selected records: `mediawork-pinterest-objects`, `music-player-state-journey`, `optical-assembly`, `polyhaven-studio-small-09-1k`. The [chrome-beetle recipe](../skills/motion-design/library/recipes/chrome-beetle.md) separates anatomy and pose from the violet studio. Proof drove wider initial framing, an in-plane membrane fold and a readable slow-motion wingbeat. A 0.55 case-opening control visibly changes the mechanism without changing the camera.

**“Make the word MELT turn into warm yellow liquid and pull itself back into letters.”**

One locally loaded Sora word sags into a connected puddle against cobalt, then recovers its glyphs. Selected records: `google-fonts-sora-variable`, `animography`, `mediawork-office-motion-tests`. The original [liquid-word recipe](../skills/motion-design/library/recipes/liquid-word.md) combines font-derived distance fields and liquid shapes. Proof found diagonal bevel quantization and detached uniform droplets; repairs use full-resolution floating-point fields and baseline-anchored drips. The alternate-word control is OOZE. This is designed liquid motion, not a fluid simulation; no Animography artwork or code is included.

**“Make a purple passionflower become a bold, wordless botanical fashion film.”**

One generated photographic-style flower supplies the full hero, pollen macro and filament crops. Selected records: `lilium-flower-family`, `ordinary-folk-dreams`. The [botanical-editorial recipe](../skills/motion-design/library/recipes/botanical-editorial.md) controls crop, strip and radial-shutter relationships. Proof supported the detailed crops but exposed a rectangular plate edge; the compositor feathers that margin. A seven-sector control varies the fourteen-sector reconstruction. These are edited views of one still, not generated biological motion or independent camera angles.
