## What transfers

- **Story before effects.** A confrontation establishes the stakes, effort and resistance develop them, a setback intensifies the conflict, and a concert montage delivers the payoff. The prompt generalizes this to a subject, goal, obstacle and outcome.
- **Variable pacing.** The 48.97-second edit contains 38 picture cuts. Several expressive shots hold for three to four seconds. The final concert passage contains nine internal cuts in about 6.77 seconds. This supports an accelerating finish, not constant rapid cutting throughout.
- **Selective typography.** White uppercase slab lettering builds source-backed dialogue phrases; red marks emphasis. Captions move between areas of negative space and disappear for the closing performance. The general prompt retains the hierarchy and collision checks, not the film’s quotes.
- **One graphic peak.** A short subject isolation on black supplies contrast. Historical masking needed manual refinement around fast arms and drumsticks. The prompt makes this conditional on clean footage and a stable matte.
- **Separate picture and sound.** The historical project independently aligned dialogue clips and a continuous music recording. The music changes pace; it is not evidence of one constant BPM. The prompt asks for phrase-aware editing, clear speech and a complete audio tail.
- **Measured finishing.** The records include frame-based picture cuts, source-word timing, caption bounds, subject-mask intersection checks, complete decoding and stream-duration checks. Those methods transfer without preserving film-specific asset acquisition or source offsets.

## What does not transfer automatically

The actors, film shots, dialogue, score, exact source intervals and reconstruction-specific crop are particular to this example. The new prompt uses the user’s subject and supplied or licensed media. It does not require the same film, track, shot count, durations or color balance. Zooms, glitches, interpolated slow motion and constant transitions were not demonstrated by this edit and are not added as generic requirements.
