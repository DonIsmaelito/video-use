# Luminous jellyfish

Original parametric anatomy with a translucent bell, fine radial ribs, four frilled oral arms and independently lagging threads. `createLuminousJellyfish({tentacleCount=18,color})` returns a Three.js `group`, `setState({pulse,phase,current})` and idempotent `dispose()`. The factory accepts4–40tentacles. The host owns the underwater stage, environment, renderer, camera, glow and time.

`pulse` clamps to0–1 and contracts the bell radius while increasing its height. `phase` moves fringes and traveling bends without accumulated state. `current` clamps to−1–1 and bends the lower appendages while preserving their anchors. All state values must be finite. The generated tube frames follow each centerline and all surfaces update in place. This is art-directed anatomy, not a hydrodynamic simulation.

The demo imports local Three.js0.186.0 and its postprocessing modules, with a Poly Haven StudioSmall09HDR used for restrained reflections. The component has no asset downloads. Keep that HDR's verified receipt with the isolated demo project; other light rigs may be supplied. The membrane uses a Fresnel-based transparent shader; the scene adds restrained bloom to its bright edges. Inspect the result at delivery size so the thin threads remain visible without turning the bell into an umbrella skeleton.

Simple prompt: “Make a translucent jellyfish pulse through an inky sea, trailing glowing ribbons.” Copy [the demo](luminous-jellyfish-demo.html) and [module](luminous-jellyfish.mjs) into an isolated project with its local dependencies and HDR. The demo exposes deterministic seek and readiness. Dispose releases the generated geometries and owned materials; do not use the handle afterward.

The delivered control changes 18 tentacles to 8 under the same camera and phase at 4.8 seconds. Both the final export and control passed four repeated backward seeks. The eight-second film passed 240-frame technical and audio validation. Proof review reduced rigid-looking ribs and excessive glow, then shortened the threads after an encoded frame exposed bottom clipping. A projection check across all 240 final poses found a minimum 34.28-pixel bottom margin, excluding bloom. This is evidence for this demo and control, not a guarantee for arbitrary camera or parameter changes. Proofs and exact prompt history are retained in the second batch source archive.
