# Small courage

An original cut-paper puppet discovers that a large circle can be rolled. Read intention through gaze, hesitant footwork, a braced lifting pose, a short retreat, purposeful pushing, and a satisfied quiet ending. The circle's stripe makes rolling observable. No text is present.

An authored numeric performance track is separate from character drawing. Shared absolute-time pose interpolation and a general two-bone inverse-kinematics solver keep limbs attached and preserve their lengths. Geometry, character proportions, paper palette, timing and expression are specific creative decisions in this example; the common runtime contains none of them. Body motion is a procedural puppet performance, not a collection of AI-generated frames or a full character-animation system.

Story: approach and size up (0–3 s), try to lift and fail (3–6 s), reconsider (6–8 s), roll instead (8–13 s), satisfaction (13–16 s). Hand reach, circle rotation, planted feet during holds and eye direction are the important review points. Original Canvas geometry and seeded paper marks; no external assets. Intentionally silent.

The appearance controls are explicit inputs, not an automatic performance redesign. Circle start/travel, radius and limb lengths are coordinated with the x/contact poses in performance.json. Substantial changes require adjusting those poses and reviewing contact. The IK primitive reports an unreachable target instead of silently stretching the limb. Palette and paper seed can change independently.
