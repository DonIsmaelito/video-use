# Open Field

Exact request lives in prompt.txt. This is an authored interpretation, not a prompt-selected scene in the runtime.

The film makes creative organization visible: independent ideas find a shared structure, then the structure becomes a brand that can inhabit different formats. A four-petal mark originates in the shape studies inside the workspace. Typography, paper, coral, leaf green, and the mark connect three deliberately different compositions.

0–3 seconds: loosely composed original note, color, shape, and typography studies float beside an editorial opening. 3–8 seconds: the same studies align into a readable workspace; a pointer gesture initiates their connection. 8–11 seconds: the workspace collapses toward a shared center and becomes an isolated brand mark. 11–13 seconds: a horizontal logo ident resolves and holds. 13–18 seconds: the mark expands into a cropped repeated field beside an editorial title, demonstrating a coordinated identity rather than a repeated layout.

Inter carries product/UI language and the wordmark; Instrument Serif carries the editorial voice. The system remains flat, crisp, and graphic. Avoid interface-sized unreadable screenshots, meaningless spinning windows, unrelated decorations, and every panel arriving by the same canned entrance.

Edit content.json to change brand, copy, card content, or palette without changing animation code. Timing and composition are authored in scene.mjs; runtime interpolation, transforms, and text fitting live in lib/motion.mjs. This specific concept belongs to the example. Infrastructure does not route arbitrary prompts into these phases.

Technical proof: exact-time forward/reverse/repeated seeks, local fonts and assets, frame-aligned delivery. Visual proof must inspect workspace legibility, card collisions during assembly, silhouette handoff into the mark, and editorial title safe areas. Silence is intentional unless an original score is added and recorded in the render contract.
