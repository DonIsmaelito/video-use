# Signal Garden creative contract

**premise**: An illustrated garden grows from sound energy: broad blue leaves respond to bass, coral flowers breathe, and lilac fans and yellow florets react to bright percussion.

**impression**: Playful, tactile, deliberate botanical graphic design rather than a music-player visualization.

**palette**: Warm paper, ink green, cobalt, coral, lemon and lilac.

**strongestFrame**: An asymmetric garden of unequal silhouettes with a large coral flower balanced by blue veined foliage.

**rhythm**: Measured cumulative energy grows the stems. Different frequency bands drive distinct kinds of movement; a central musical quiet passage lets the garden settle before fuller growth and final release.

**type**: No added labels or title cards.

**assets**: Original Canvas geometry and an original synthesized stereo score. No external imagery, fonts or music.

**failureConditions**: A hidden BPM grid drives motion instead of the decoded audio; Every plant simply scales together; Decorative labels compensate for an uninteresting composition

The audio analysis is reusable infrastructure. Garden geometry, score composition and aesthetic mappings are example-specific authored choices. Replaying source yields the same frames; repeating the natural-language prompt alone does not guarantee identical art direction.

Repair: Changed initial vertical squash to uniform growth to preserve leaf and flower silhouettes.
